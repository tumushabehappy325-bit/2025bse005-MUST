How to Apply

Follow these steps to complete your application.

  1. Create an account with your names and email. A temporary password is sent to you.
  2. Sign in with your email and the temporary password.
  3. Fill in the application form: programme choices, personal details, O and A level results.
  4. Upload your result slips, pay the application fee and submit.
